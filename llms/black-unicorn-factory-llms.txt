# Black Unicorn Factory

> Black Unicorn Factory (BUF) is a Los Angeles based pre-IPO business accelerator that prepares companies led by founders from underrepresented communities for direct listings on national stock exchanges, positioning equity rather than debt as the route to capital. It publishes no public API, developer portal, SDKs, or machine-readable API contract.

## Programs

- [New Black Wall Street Pre-IPO Program](https://blackunicornfactory.com/new-black-wall-street/): The pre-IPO direct listing acceleration program.
- [Follow Me For Equity](https://followmeforequity.com/): Platform (iOS and Android apps) where individuals earn equity in pre-IPO companies by completing verified social engagement tasks instead of investing cash.
- [Follow Me For Equity registration](https://followmeforequity.com/student-registration/): Sign up for the program.

## APIs

Black Unicorn Factory publishes no public API. As of 2026-08-02 there is no
OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, AsyncAPI, MCP server, A2A agent card, developer
portal, SDK, or `/.well-known/` discovery document on any company host
(blackunicornfactory.com, followmeforequity.com). The only machine-readable
endpoint present is the incidental WordPress REST API root at
https://blackunicornfactory.com/wp-json/, which is content-management plumbing
for the marketing site rather than a product API, and is therefore not
catalogued as a Black Unicorn Factory API.
